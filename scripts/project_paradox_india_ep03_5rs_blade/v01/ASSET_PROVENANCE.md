# Assets and provenance

All four stages, character assemblies, coin/blade/press geometry, procedural
materials and synthesized foley/music are authored for this production.
Generic geometry constructors were adapted from the existing Gold Mine helper.

The two imported stalls in public/bridge/kenney are generated local assets:
stall-red and stall-green are explicitly defined in scripts/generate-kenney-glb.mjs.
They are not claimed to be downloaded Kenney originals or independently verified
CC0 files. Their copied materials/images are sanitized in this production.
No external model download was used for this version.

Narration: user-supplied MP3; identity and decoded length in input_manifest.json.
No replacement narration or time stretching. Original decoded WAV is preserved.
Windows Nirmala UI is used for physical world prop labels only. No subtitles.
